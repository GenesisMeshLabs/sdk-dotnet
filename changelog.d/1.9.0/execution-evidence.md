### Added

- **Execution evidence.** `ExecutionRecorder` builds and signs the record a controller submits after acting on an authorized decision, as the Python reference `record_execution` does: the same canonical form and signature, checked against records the reference signed. Records chain under their decision (`PriorRecord`) and on their resource across decisions (`PriorResource`; `ExecutionRecorder.HeadOf` and `Digest`). `executed_at` is now, but never before the decision. Secret material (`SecretMaterialException`) and values the NA would refuse as `invalid_json` (`StrictJsonException`) are refused before anything is signed. `ISigner` signs for an executor key too.
- **The evidence store client.** `client.EvidenceStore` submits a signed record (`Submit`, `POST /evidence/execution`; a resubmission is `duplicate`), reads where a resource's chain ends (`ResourceHead`, `StoredResourceHead`), searches stored entries (`Search`, and `Iterate` over the pages), gives each resource's latest change from its execution evidence and break-glass records (`ResourceStates`), and registers executor keys, optionally limited to a resource prefix (`RegisterExecutorKey`, privileged).
- **Offline checks.** `OfflineVerifier.VerifyExecutionSignature` checks a record under its executor's key. `VerifyJustificationSignature` checks an evaluation's justification proof under the NA's keys, or under the key of its history valid when the proof was issued. `ExecutionDigest` gives a record's digest. Each refuses a record with a field this SDK does not know or a form the reference does not write.
- **`Canonical.Timestamp`** writes a time as the NA does: UTC, microseconds when not zero, `Z`.
